Building a playable tic tac toe game

Some sort of fun quirk either for technical demonstration or fun/wow factor

As quickly playable as possible.

Written in Go. Ebiten game engine

"Playable" here means in-browser. Host it on my personal website. Fallback to itch.io


# What's the game look like?

Idler style tic-tac-toe. Moves are made randomly and by clicking/spacebar/etc. X wins, O wins and Draws are tracked. Raw number of games will be the currency for upgrades.

"more tic" -> you pay a number of games and moves are made automatically at 1/second, 2/second, 3/second

"more tac" -> Duplicates the board, clicks still randomly make 1 move on one board, but "more tic" makes moves across all boards

"more toe" -> cosmetic only, expensive and does something silly on the screen. Buy it 3-5 times and game is over.
